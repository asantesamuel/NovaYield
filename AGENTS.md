# NovaYield Project Context

Before changing NovaYield requirements, Jira tickets, architecture, or implementation, read [novayield-research-and-product-decisions.md](novayield-research-and-product-decisions.md), especially §6. It is the canonical record of Product Owner decisions 1–9 and the assumptions that remain provisional.

- Treat the finalized decisions in §6 as the approved MVP baseline unless the Product Owner explicitly changes them or new evidence materially warrants review.
- Do not treat Product Owner approval of a direction as local agronomic validation. Decision 9 defers expert review; pilot-specific crop traits, calendars, and agronomic rules remain provisional until validated.
- Keep unsupported traits and measurements unknown; do not infer crop benefits, field conditions, or validated recommendation eligibility from broad desk research.
- When new evidence conflicts with the baseline, explain the evidence and implementation impact, flag the affected assumption for review, and avoid silently changing the approved product behavior.
- Keep future Jira tickets and implementation plans consistent with the decision record, including its indicator, scoring, missing-evidence, and NASA snapshot freshness policies.
