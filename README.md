# NovaYield

NovaYield is a web-first decision-support prototype that helps farmers and agricultural extension officers explore crop-rotation options under changing environmental conditions.

The project is being developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It combines NASA-derived environmental context with farmer-provided field information, crop characteristics, and user priorities to compare plausible rotation strategies and explain their trade-offs.

## What it does

The planned MVP guides a user through a simple workflow:

1. Enter a field location and relevant field details, such as soil texture, irrigation access, previous crops, and priorities.
2. Review historical precipitation and temperature context from NASA POWER, along with clearly defined environmental indicators.
3. Compare three candidate crop rotations across three consecutive planting seasons, including a baseline option when current practice is known.
4. See why each option was included, how it compares on key criteria, and what assumptions or risks apply.

## MVP focus

The prototype will focus on one Ghanaian pilot area and a small set of locally relevant crops. The exact pilot area, crop calendar, crop rules, and scoring assumptions are still being finalized and require local agronomic review.

NovaYield is intended to support discussion and decision-making. Its ratings are relative, rule-based indicators; they are not yield forecasts, soil tests, profitability estimates, or guaranteed planting advice. NASA data provides area-level environmental context and may not represent conditions in an individual field.

## Project status

NovaYield is in the early MVP planning and development stage. The team is aligning the pilot scenario and validating the scope before finalizing implementation details.
